Tiny KV store in Go with mmap

SEP 14, 2026 · 8 MIN · github ↗

Why a toy KV?

Reading about LSMs is abstract. Writing 300 lines of append-only log makes compaction viscerally obvious. This is that project.

Log format

Records are length-prefixed, CRC-checked. On boot we scan once. Reads are a single mmap slice.

Numbers

Gets at ~650ns, puts at ~2.9µs on M1. Fine for <100k keys. Part 2: compaction without stopping the world.
